Every bolt can break

Each module has its own health. Shoot off a gun and it falls to the floor. Break a chassis and the robot limps. Break the head and the controls go haywire.

Build anything

Pick a frame, an engine, a shell, batteries and a head, then fill every arm with a weapon and every pod with a gadget. Each part drops onto the turntable as you fit it.

  1. Frame + engineLegs, wheels, tracks or hover
  2. Shell + batteriesArmour, arm count and power
  3. HeadSensors, and it looks at you
  4. ArmsUp to four guns, left and right triggers
  5. GadgetsMines, turrets, cloaks and radar
  6. Now swap anythingEvery part comes off again

Meet the scrap heap

Wheels, legs, tracks, a hover raft or one giant roller. Bolt on up to four guns, two gadgets, a battery and a head. Hover a robot to turn it around.

Garage, workbench, arena

  1. Buy the parts

    Frames, shells, weapon mechanisms, barrels, batteries, heads and gadgets. Your level unlocks more.

  2. Bolt it together

    Turn parts into modules on the workbench, stack them into a robot and watch the weight limit.

  3. Take it to the arena

    One life per round. Team Deathmatch, Battery Run, Bomb Squad or King of the Yard, against bots or friends online.

Five places to lose a wheel

Every arena is about 200 × 144 m with three levels: a sunken floor, the street and the rooftops. Ramps, lifts, jump pads, buildings you can drive into, and props you can blow up.

I missed a game.
So I rebuilt it.

A long time ago I played Guns and Robots with my friends, and those are some of my best gaming memories. When it was gone, I wrote to the developers. Nobody wrote back.

Hi, I'm Dimitar. I loved that game, so I started building one from scratch: every model, sound and map in BOLTYARD is made in code, no assets borrowed.

It's grown into something real, and I'd like to make it better with other people. If you played Guns and Robots, want to help, or just like robots that fall apart, say hi.

If you worked on the original game, I'd especially love to hear from you.

Questions

What is BOLTYARD?

A fan-made robot arena game that runs in the browser. You build a robot from parts in a garage (chassis, body, head, up to four weapons and two devices), then fight team battles of up to 5v5 in Team Deathmatch, Battery Run, Bomb Squad and King of the Yard.

Is it a Guns and Robots remake?

It's inspired by Guns and Robots, but it isn't a copy and isn't affiliated with that game's makers. Every model, texture, sound, map and line of code was made from scratch.

Can I play it?

Not publicly yet. It's in development and being tested with friends on a private server. Get in touch if you'd like to try it.

How can I help?

Coders (TypeScript, Three.js), 3D and sound artists, level designers, testers and old Guns and Robots pilots are all welcome. Join the Discord, open an issue or send a message, and I'll add you to the game repository.

What is it built with?

TypeScript, Vite, Three.js for rendering and Rapier for physics, plus a Node.js server for online battles. Meshes, textures, sounds and music are all generated in code, so the game ships without a single art file.
